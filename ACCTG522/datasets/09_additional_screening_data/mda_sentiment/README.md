# MD&A narrative and sentiment/complexity scores

Summary file is provided for screening, text-only processed MD&A sections are provided as firm-year files as well. Summary CSV files are UTF-8 without BOM, comma-delimited, with quoted/escaped fields as needed. In Alteryx choose UTF-8 and import CIK/accession/ticker as strings to preserve leading zeros. Narrative paths are provided on OneDrive due to the size of the files. Narratives can be used for extra analysis but are not required to recompute provided measures.

Text bytes are copied without summaries, metadata prefixes or reformatting from the explicit central sentiment input. Paragraphs and punctuation remain. The numbered Item 7 heading is excluded just as in the existing scoring pipeline. Prose in layout tables is retained under the structural policy; genuine numeric tables are excluded. Original filings, table fragments, readable HTML, detailed word/sentence audits and diagnostics stay centrally and are not part of this compact package. These are included in the full filings packages, which are much larger files.

Readability applies an extra prose selection: short headings/bullet fragments and recognized page furniture are excluded; prose needs at least five alphabetic runs and punctuation, and retained Syntok sentences need three readability words. Readability compounds/apostrophes are single words while sentiment uses alphabetic runs. Syllables use LM counts, bundled CMUdict and a recorded minimum-one fallback. Thus readability W differs from sentiment n_word_tokens. The exported full narrative remains unchanged; readability does not use every word in it.

Six LM categories use active flags >0, including negative removal-marker handling. Counts repeat exact matches without stemming, stopwords or automatic negation adjustment; categories can overlap. The fixed Loughran-McDonald_MasterDictionary_1993-2025.csv dictionary is applied across periods (SHA-256 e2d1328682bab7d2187684fb9f5420bb730401c9eefc00daf835edd203f4859d). This is not historical dictionary reconstruction or a reconstructed historical investable universe. Shares are fractions; net tone can be negative; undefined denominators are blank. It shares the same SHA as the dictionary available at the original authors' site: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1iq2RUf8qGFEAk1g8wQntP3habOnR3fXF/view?usp=sharing 

The LM sentiment measures are based on the paper LOUGHRAN, T. and MCDONALD, B. (2011), When Is a Liability Not a Liability? Textual Analysis, Dictionaries, and 10-Ks. The Journal of Finance, 66: 35-65. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01625.x (uw credentials/on campus might be required for access).

gunning_fog_simplified counts all >=3-syllable words without exclusions for names/compounds/suffixes. Higher Fog/grade means harder estimated reading; higher Flesch Reading Ease means easier reading. Flesch is not clamped, meaning it can have values outside of the range 0-100 (i.e., negative vales and values greater than 100). These are readability proxies, not complete business complexity measures. Uncertainty extraction status is separate from LM uncertainty counts.
